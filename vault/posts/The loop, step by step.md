This post is an example of an *experience*. The words you're reading live in an Obsidian note and the layout lives in `src/experiences/loop.astro`. Each `##` heading in the note becomes one step, and each step's title becomes a label on the ring. Scroll to move through it.

## Sense

Every loop starts by looking at the world as it is, not as you expect it to be. A thermostat reads the room. A writer rereads the draft.

## Compare

A reading means nothing alone. It has to be held against a goal, and the gap between the two is the only thing the loop cares about.

## Decide

The gap suggests a change. A good loop makes small changes, because a large correction tends to overshoot and has to be corrected in turn.

## Act

The change goes out into the world, where it meets everything else that's happening. Its effect is never quite what was planned.

## Learn

So the loop looks again. This is where it stops being a line and closes into a circle, which is the whole idea. See [[How this blog works]] for how this post is put together.
